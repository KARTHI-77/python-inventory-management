Optimization
------------
The original calculate_inventory_value() method iterated
through every product and calculated price * quantity on
every call.

The optimized implementation maintains a cached
_total_inventory_value and updates it incrementally when
products are added, removed, or their stock quantity changes.

JSON loading resets the cached total and rebuilds it through
the existing add_product() validation path.

Profiling confirmed that the original builtins.sum()
and generator expression were the primary bottleneck.
After optimization, calculate_inventory_value() performs
an O(1) cached-value lookup.
